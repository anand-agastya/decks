# Al Yamamah University — InsightVerge Applied AI Partnership

**Strategic Concept Note | August 2026**

*AI Skill Weeks, Executive Academies, Applied AI Centre of Excellence and Academic Pathways for Saudi Arabia*

---

## Slide 1 — Core Proposition

Build market traction first through Skill Weeks and executive offerings; then stack evidence into certificates, professional credentials, AI-for-X pathways and later advanced academic programs.

---

## Slide 2 — CoE Governance and Roles

Clear ownership preserves academic sovereignty while bringing specialist AI capability and industry relevance.

**Al Yamamah University:**
- Own academic governance, approvals, awards and institutional infrastructure
- Nominate faculty and students; activate local enterprise relationships
- Own institutional outcome reporting and learner records

**InsightVerge:**
- Design/refresh curricula, labs, rubrics and technical standards
- Lead initial specialist delivery, faculty enablement, project architecture and QA
- Support dashboards, capstone governance and advanced content refresh

**Industry participants:**
- Validate industry relevance and contribute practitioner judgment
- Provide problem statements, domain context, mentors

## Slide 5 — Academic Expansion Runway

Use market evidence, CoE quality and faculty capability before committing to larger academic programs.

| Phase | Offering | Evidence needed |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| Now | Skill Weeks + Executive Academy | Immediate traction across students and enterprises |
| Next | Micro-credentials + Certificates | Visible progression and portfolio evidence |
| Then | PGP / Professional Diploma | Serious applied AI transition credential |
| Later | Master's + Executive AI Pathways | Advanced systems + working-professional options |
| Future | Bachelor-level AI / AI-for-X expansion | Only after demand, approval and faculty maturity |

AI-for-X: Software, networks/security, industrial engineering, business, finance, legal, operations and architecture can all receive applied AI pathways without waiting for a new degree.

## Slide 6 — Why This Partnership Now?

**National pull:** Saudi Data and AI strategy emphasizes workforce transformation, practical capability, responsible use and sectoral adoption.

**YU platform:** Engineering, cybersecurity and executive education create a credible academic and professional base for applied AI.

**Market gap:** Many learners know AI concepts; fewer can build, deploy, govern and defend real AI systems.

**Need:** What is needed is an applied AI capability platform — not a generic training menu.

## Slide 8 — Portfolio Architecture

Use the catalog as a ladder: short evidence-producing programs first, then credentials and academic pathways.

1. **Applied AI Skill Weeks** — One week = One capability + one defensible artifact
2. **Certificate Pathways** — Evidence-based ladder
3. **Executive AI Academy** — Leaders + functions + sectors
4. **AI Centre of Excellence** — Problem bank + artifact QA
5. **Academic Pathways** — Diploma, master's, research

Immediate launch: Skill Weeks, executive cohorts and CoE/Studio pilots.
Scaling: Certificates, professional credentials and academic pathways, based on artifact quality, learner demand and employer feedback.

## Slide 10 — Stackable Pathways

Skill Weeks become a credential ladder that uses completion evidence, not attendance alone.

| Credential | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Skill Week (5 days) | One capability + artifact |
| Foundation Certificate | 2-3 Skill Weeks — AI foundations, productivity and responsible use |
| Advanced Certificate | 4-5 Skill Weeks + project — AI engineering, GenAI or secure-AI portfolio |
| Professional Credential (PGP/Diploma) | Studio-backed prototype, system card and defense |
| Master's / Research Pathway | Later — Advanced systems + applied research evidence |

Credentials awarded based on Skill Week completion, assessed artifacts, reviewer feedback and capstone defense. This makes the certificate employer-visible and evidence-based.
