## Three Differentiation Buckets (Murthy's ask)

### 1. Training Academy
- **Train-the-Administrator** — Three-tier model: Executive AI awareness for senior civil servants, AI project governance for mid-level managers, hands-on engineering for technical cadre
- **Continuous certification** — Micro-credentials that follow the person, not the role (officers rotate every 2-3 years)
- **Evidence-based credentialing** — Each completion produces an artifact (risk assessment, prototype, policy memo)

### 2. Research Innovation
- **Policy-to-prototype pipeline** — Policy document → identify AI opportunity → working prototype in 6 weeks → evaluate → recommend
- **Domain-specific AI research units** — Embedded teams in health, agriculture, education, urban planning
- **Regulatory sandbox** — Controlled environment to test AI systems before deployment at scale

### 3. Command Analytics Centre
- **AI project portfolio dashboard** — Track health, maturity, vendor performance, outcomes across all government AI initiatives
- **Alert-and-escalation framework** — Automatic flagging against governance playbook
- **AI-SDLC audit integration** — Every project passes through defined audit gates (requirements → design → data → build → deploy → monitor)
- **Real-time national AI pulse** — Connected dashboard showing what's working, stalled, where bottlenecks are
